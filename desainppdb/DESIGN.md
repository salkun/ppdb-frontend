# Design System

## Brand & Style
The design system adopts a modern, reliable, and professional corporate aesthetic, utilizing clean lines, balanced proportions, and high-fidelity visual feedback.

## Colors
- **Color Mode:** Light
- **Primary Color:** `#1275e2`
- **Secondary Color:** `#5f78a3`
- **Tertiary Color:** `#c55b00`
- **Neutral Color:** `#74777f`

## Typography
- **Headline Font:** Chivo
- **Body Font:** Inter
- **Label Font:** Inter

## Layout & Spacing
Maintains a consistent spacing scale and fluid grid structure designed for high legibility and balanced density across devices.

## Elevation & Depth
Utilizes tonal surface variations and soft ambient shadows to establish clear visual hierarchy.

## Shapes
Components feature a rounded design language with consistent radii (`roundedness: 2`) providing a friendly yet professional finish.

## Components
Standardized components including buttons, inputs, cards, and chips follow the established color, typography, and shape tokens for cohesive user experience.
